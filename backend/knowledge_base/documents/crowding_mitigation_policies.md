# Emergency Department Crowding Mitigation Policies & Surge Management

## 1. Capacity Operational States & Thresholds

### Operational Capacity Classification
1. **Normal Capacity (Occupancy < 75%)**
   - Standard staffing allocation and room assignments.
   - Routine laboratory and radiology turnaround times.

2. **Elevated Capacity / Moderate Surge (Occupancy 75% – 85%)**
   - Preliminary surge warning issued to inpatient unit charge nurses.
   - Protocolized diagnostic ordering at triage enabled (e.g., standard X-rays, basic lab panels).

3. **High Capacity / Heavy Crowding (Occupancy 85% – 95%)**
   - Full Capacity Protocol Activation: Inpatient units expedite pending discharge decisions.
   - Auxiliary hallway treatment spots enabled for stable ESI 3 patients awaiting bed disposition.
   - Boarding Management: Designated holding area established for admitted patients awaiting inpatient bed transfer.

4. **Critical Capacity / Severe Surge (Occupancy > 95%)**
   - Code Yellow / Red Surge Threshold: Chief Medical Officer and Nursing Supervisor notified.
   - Regional EMS notified of temporary ambulance diversion status (subject to trauma center regulations).
   - Elective inpatient admissions temporarily paused to clear inpatient bed capacity.

---

## 2. Key Operational Performance Indicators
- **Inpatient Boarding Time**: Average time admitted patients spend in ER beds awaiting inpatient bed transfer. Target: < 2 hours. Exceeding 4 hours triggers Full Capacity Protocol.
- **Left Without Being Seen (LWBS) Rate**: Target < 2.0% of total arrivals. LWBS > 3.5% triggers triage staffing reinforcement.
- **Door-to-Provider Time**: Target < 30 minutes for ESI 3, < 10 minutes for ESI 2.
