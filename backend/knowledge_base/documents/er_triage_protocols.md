# Emergency Department Triage Protocols & ESI Guidelines

## 1. Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Overview
The Emergency Severity Index (ESI) is a five-level triage algorithm that categorizes emergency department patients by evaluating clinical acuity and anticipated resource utilization.

### ESI Triage Levels

#### ESI Level 1: Resuscitation (Immediate Life Threat)
- **Clinical Description**: Severe, immediate life-threatening condition requiring immediate life-saving intervention.
- **Examples**: Cardiac arrest, respiratory arrest, severe anaphylaxis, unresponsiveness, acute respiratory failure.
- **Target Wait Time**: 0 minutes (Immediate bed placement in resuscitation bay).

#### ESI Level 2: Emergent (High Risk / Time-Sensitive)
- **Clinical Description**: High-risk situation, confused/disoriented/lethargic patient, or severe pain/distress.
- **Examples**: Active chest pain, stroke symptoms, acute severe asthma, ectopic pregnancy suspicion, acute psychosis.
- **Target Wait Time**: < 10–15 minutes.

#### ESI Level 3: Urgent (Multiple Resources Needed)
- **Clinical Description**: Vital signs are stable, but patient requires 2 or more resources (e.g., labs, X-ray, IV fluids).
- **Examples**: Abdominal pain, high fever with cough, moderate laceration requiring repair.
- **Target Wait Time**: < 30–60 minutes.

#### ESI Level 4: Less Urgent (One Resource Needed)
- **Clinical Description**: Stable condition requiring only 1 diagnostic or therapeutic resource.
- **Examples**: Simple ankle sprain, uncomplicated laceration, sore throat with throat swab.
- **Target Wait Time**: < 60–120 minutes.

#### ESI Level 5: Non-Urgent (No Resources Needed)
- **Clinical Description**: Stable condition requiring physical examination or re-evaluation only, without diagnostic testing.
- **Examples**: Suture removal, prescription renewal, minor chronic skin rash.
- **Target Wait Time**: < 120 minutes.

## 2. Re-Triage & Vital Sign Escalation Triggers
- **Re-Assessment Interval**: Every 30 minutes for ESI Level 3; every 60 minutes for ESI Levels 4 and 5.
- **Vital Sign Triggers**:
  - Heart Rate > 110 bpm or < 50 bpm -> Escalate triage assessment.
  - Respiratory Rate > 24 bpm or SpO2 < 92% -> Immediate nurse evaluation.
  - Systolic BP < 90 mmHg or > 180 mmHg -> Escalate to ESI Level 2 evaluation.
